## Design Context

### Users
Personal photography enthusiasts using a Nikon Z6III on macOS. They connect the camera or card-backed camera storage to browse photos directly before deciding what to import, keep, or rate.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, photographic, focused. The interface should feel closer to a calm photo review desk than a technical device utility.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a quiet professional photo-browser direction with some Apple Photos ease. Prioritize large imagery, restrained controls, readable metadata, and low visual noise. Avoid loud gradients, dense camera-control dashboards, decorative chrome, and generic SaaS card grids.

### Design Principles
- Photos are the primary surface; UI recedes until needed.
- Browsing should feel fast, calm, and spatially stable.
- Rating is a first-class action with immediate visual feedback.
- Metadata should support decisions without competing with the image.
- Native hardware states should be explicit: no camera, loading, unsupported write-back, and write failure.
